ADDITIONAL REVENUE CHANNELS

More ways to support the app

These are planned business models, not active products, offers or paid recommendations. Safety and unbiased diagnostics come first.

01 · ONE-TIME PURCHASE

Deep diagnosis credits

Sell a small pack of advanced research requests to occasional users without requiring a subscription. Meter actual costs and expire credits only under clearly disclosed terms.

02 · PROFESSIONAL DOCUMENTS

Repair reports

Charge for optional professionally formatted, exportable inspection and repair-planning reports. Basic plain-text exports stay free.

03 · SERVICE MARKETPLACE

Shop referrals

Optional, clearly labeled referral fees when a customer chooses a participating mechanic. Never change diagnostic rankings based on payment.

04 · PARTS PARTNERSHIPS

Affiliate parts links

Earn a disclosed commission on eligible purchases. Show verified fitment and independent alternatives, including non-affiliate options.

05 · VEHICLE OWNERSHIP

Fleet management

Offer paid maintenance tracking, vehicle inspections and service reminders for delivery operators, independent fleets and used-car dealers.

06 · WORKSHOP SOFTWARE

Team seats

Per-seat professional access with role-based controls, customer consent and shared work orders after secure accounts are ready.

07 · EDUCATION

Guided repair courses

Original paid courses, diagnostic training and skill assessments. Keep essential safety instructions accessible without payment.

08 · VEHICLE HISTORY

Pre-purchase inspection kits

Paid, structured checklists and report templates for buyers. Do not represent an AI assessment as a physical inspection.

09 · BUSINESS TO BUSINESS

API licensing

Usage-based diagnostic workflow access for authorized automotive software partners, with strict quotas and no resale of personal vehicle records.

Why not charge yet?

Subscriptions should only launch after reliable AI responses, video uploads, real accounts, billing security, usage metering and clear cancellation controls are implemented and tested. Prices and feature limits above are proposals, not offers or guarantees.
