Privacy and disclosures

Last revised: October 12, 2026. This page describes the current beta implementation. It is informational and should be reviewed for legal compliance before a broad commercial launch.

Information kept on your device

Vehicle profiles, mileage entries, repair notes, saved conversations and diagnostic reports are currently stored in your browser’s local storage. They are not synced between devices, are not tied to a formal user account, and may disappear if you clear site data or change browsers. Avoid storing sensitive information you do not want preserved locally.

Information sent to our server and AI processor

When you submit a diagnostic chat, the prompt, selected vehicle context, recent conversation messages and any photo or video attachment are transmitted to our application server and to a third-party AI provider (currently Google Gemini) for generation. Video files are uploaded through a server-side gateway to the provider’s temporary file-processing service. Uploaded files have limited retention according to provider rules; your browser may retain conversation references until you delete them.

Do not upload license plates, registration records, addresses, faces, medical information, or other identifying information unless absolutely needed. Crop or conceal personal details first. We do not currently provide a dedicated user-data download or deletion portal; you can delete locally saved content by deleting conversations, garage entries or browser site data. Server/provider retention depends on their terms and infrastructure logging.

Hosting and operational logs

Our website is hosted through Vercel. Standard HTTP requests may produce operational logs including IP addresses, access timestamps, errors and request metadata. The app has limited per-instance request throttling to reduce abuse. A green “configured” state means required settings are present; it does not guarantee a successful AI response.

Advertising

Eligible public educational pages may show clearly labeled advertising after approval by an advertising platform. The interactive diagnostic chat is not currently configured to carry display advertising. If ads are enabled, advertising technology providers may process device information, cookies or identifiers in accordance with their own notices and applicable consent requirements. Appropriate consent-management arrangements must be put in place before targeted ads are enabled where required by law.

Accuracy and safety

This service is intended to help drivers think through automotive symptoms. AI and guided workflows can make mistakes and are not a substitute for a qualified technician, verified manufacturer specifications or an in-person safety inspection. For impaired brakes or steering, overheating, a fuel leak, smoke or other hazardous conditions, stop driving safely and seek professional assistance.

Contact and feedback

For technical feedback or privacy requests while the service is in beta, use the project’s public issue tracker. Do not post passwords, API keys, VINs or other personal information in public issues. A private support channel should be established before the site expands to general audiences.
