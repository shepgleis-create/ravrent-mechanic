## Key product boundaries
- This is a **local browser beta**. Exported backups may contain sensitive customer names, VINs and conversations; handle them securely. Backups imported into nonempty datasets are intentionally **skipped**; record-by-record merges must use each tool's native import.
- A Garage vehicle with no valid VIN cannot be linked to workshop/reseller/Casebook entries by model name; status counts remain conservative.
- Safety/case status is user-entered and is never an independent roadworthiness certificate.
- The quoted pipeline is not cash revenue, payment settlement or profit.
- AI provider access and models listing is not a verified generated reply.
- The public paid-plan cards are proposals only; no subscriptions or feature entitlements are active.
- `/ads.txt` and guide placement code do **not** mean ads are actively serving.
